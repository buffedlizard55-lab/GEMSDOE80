These are falsifiable hypotheses, not established evidence of geothermal vents. The prediction target is faults, not vent locations or a guarantee of geothermal productivity. A strain gradient can identify an interpolation artefact just as readily as a fault; a conductivity anomaly need not indicate hot fluid.

## Required validation before any implementation or promotion

1. Start a separately authorized budget; preserve the current negative card and pre-register the exact three variants and feature transformations.
2. Implement a shared geographically blocked split with whole fault components and buffers. Fit every catalogue-dependent transformation on visible faults only. Include convolution halos in exclusion buffers, and verify that changing hidden labels cannot change derived features.
3. Run feature-alone canaries, including transformed features, visible-catalogue distance, and the combined surface. Investigate AUC above 0.90 (also inspect inverse-predictive features); low AUC is not proof of absence of leakage.
4. Evaluate a current-best baseline using the identical folds, eligible area, emission budget, and evaluator. Require improvement over that baseline, not merely random dots. Provide paired spatial uncertainty; fixed-prediction bootstrap alone is insufficient for a promotion claim.
5. Obtain a complete versioned registry. Test the surface before placement and final-dot ranks/3-pixel overlap after placement. Stop and record duplicate if either threshold is exceeded. The historical three-reference check does not prove uniqueness against every listed site.
6. Only a separate selector may approve a weekly slot after verifying the current competition cap. Then write via the shared writer, validate re-read bytes against an authenticated official template, and preserve the organizer receipt after upload.

No official external source availability is claimed beyond the pinned organizer GitHub repository. New LiDAR, InSAR, thermal or groundwater ideas are deliberately not proposed as currently viable because their official downloads, coverage and licenses could not be checked within the available network access.
