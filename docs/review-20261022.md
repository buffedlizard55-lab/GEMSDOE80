## Pass 2 — bugs and assumptions repaired

1. The shared bootstrap sampled components with replacement then used `unique()`, discarding multiplicity while retaining full false-positive mass. Replaced it with additive 4×4 spatial-block resampling, preserving cross-boundary kernel credit before partitioning and recomputing pooled ratios. The interval is **conditional on fixed predictions and split**; frequentist coverage and model/generalization uncertainty are not established.
2. The split is random whole connected components with a buffer, **not geographically blocked**. Corrected labels and blocked promotion. A low AUC alone does not prove absence of leakage.
3. The format validator had a private split and bootstrapped one scalar, implying a spurious degenerate interval. It now uses the shared split and reports a static-artifact diagnostic with `ci95: null`, not HOLDOUT-DTI validation.
4. The standalone binary evaluation CLI silently thresholded probability values. It now rejects nonbinary values and mismatched grids and labels static-raster scoring diagnostic-only: a TIFF cannot prove its features were rebuilt without hidden faults.
5. Numeric nodata is no longer included in the shared template footprint merely because it is finite. Label NaNs are excluded.
6. The shared binary writer rejects nonfinite, fractional and incorrectly shaped masks instead of silently converting them to boolean predictions.
7. Empty registries fail closed. Duplicate checks now include final-dot rank correlation as well as surface rank correlation and 3-pixel overlap. The legacy runner checks the surface before placement, stops on final duplicates, persists the exhausted-budget stop, removes the fallback to failed arms, and no longer ranks arms by SGMC diagnostics.
8. Replaced “will not be rejected” and “promote” public claims with **format pass / organizer acceptance unconfirmed / negative**. Corrected both run cards and exposed audit links at the top of Pages.

## Why sparse H33-style dots can help — and what is not known

Under the repository's implemented metric, truth credit is the maximum kernel-weighted nearby prediction, whereas surplus probability mass can add false-positive cost. Sparse coverage can therefore outperform a thick ridge without improving the underlying detector. Catalogue-near pruning can remove redundant mass if those traces are excluded from test labels. These are mechanistic explanations, **not causal attribution of H33's exact organizer score**. Missing truth, receipt and controlled organizer ablations prevent that conclusion. Low catalogue holdout performance cannot be equated with hidden-fault leaderboard performance, and faults are not equivalent to geothermal vents.
