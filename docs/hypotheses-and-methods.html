3. Architecture Summary of the 5-Model Cross-Fitted ADPT-80 Ensemble

Inside each Leave-One-Quadrant-Out (LOQO) spatial fold (NW, NE, SW, SE), training data is strictly restricted to the complementary three quadrants (assert not any(train & test_region) and assert not any(train & withheld_truth)), expanded across 5 independent synthetic whole-component hiding worlds, and fit using five structurally diverse gradient-boosted architectures:

  • Model 1 (lgbm_leaf): Leaf-wise regularized GBDT on all 176 channels (num_leaves=63, colsample_bytree=0.58, subsample=0.80).
  • Model 2 (lgbm_et): Randomized split-threshold Extra-Trees GBDT on all 176 channels (extra_trees=True, num_leaves=95, colsample_bytree=0.45).
  • Model 3 (lgbm_goss_deep): Gradient-based One-Side Sampling (GOSS) deep interaction booster (boosting_type='goss', num_leaves=95, reg_alpha=3.5, reg_lambda=12.0).
  • Models 4 & 5 (lgbm_phys, et_phys): Pure zero-catalogue blind physical specialists trained strictly on the first 162 pure geoscience channels with zero fault-network inputs, blended explicitly into greenfield basin zones (d_cat > 12 px) so blind basin discoveries rely directly on geophysical half-space signatures.
