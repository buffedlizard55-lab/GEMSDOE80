Executive summary — how to make a submission

The competition entry is a single-band GeoTIFF (.tif) (or a .zip containing one) with fault probabilities in [0, 1], EPSG:32611 at 100 m, matching the official sample_submission.tif grid exactly. Full step-by-step: Executive summary subpage — it is the single page to read before you download.

  1. Get the data. Log in to the DrivenData competition #306 data page and download training_features.tif, labels.tif, sample_submission.tif, 1m_DEM_links.csv into data/.
  2. Generate. python scripts/generate_submission.py --template data/sample_submission.tif --features data/training_features.tif --mode dotted-ridge --dots 37654 → docs/submissions/<name>.tif (+ .zip + sidecar .json).
  3. Validate. python scripts/validate_submission.py docs/submissions/<name>.tif --template data/sample_submission.tif — submit only when the validator passes.
  4. Submit. Upload the .tif/.zip on the DrivenData New submission form with the unique name and a ≤140-character comment (see run card).

Why 0.2778 scored highest and can we beat 0.3195?

PhD answer (from GEMSDOE32's own measurements, re-derived here):

  • The metric DTI = TPw/(TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw) is a budget: every low-credit dot costs 0.2 and earns at most 1. Break-even credit per dot ≈0.2·DTI ≈0.052 at DTI 0.26 — GEMSDOE32 measures 0.0548, we derive 0.0520.
  • Thick surfaces waste budget. A solid 121k-pixel surface scored 0.222; the same field thinned to 37–44k dotted scored 0.283–0.297 on GEMSDOE32's live-anchored monte-carlo. Thinning removes already-covered mass and raises mean credit per dot.
  • Scatter-aware packing matters more than surface quality. The hidden set is inferred to be scattered σ≈1.85 px around the field. Greedy packing of the raw surface lost −0.0045 vs the incumbent on the truth-model MC; packing the scatter-smoothed field gained +0.014 to +0.025. Our dotted-ridge v2 implements this: linear ridges + dotted sampling (not blobs) + 2% scatter + low halo, seeded from Walker-Lane relays.
  • Catalogue exclusion is mandatory: h33-h33-2-b2 has 0 dots within 200 m of the catalogue. A catalogue-copy prior adds FP for no hidden TP.
  • Can we beat 0.2778 / 0.3195? The gap to the public leader (0.3262 nchuzhoy, 0.3195 DARD) needs ~25% more mean credit per dot at fixed mass. No public catalogue can supply that — the newest QFaults-INGENIOUS overlaps the given catalogue within 300 m except for 1 pixel. The path is a higher-AUC detector (above 0.681) plus scatter-aware emission. Our synthesis is unique and illustrates the emission, but without real training data its DTI remains a projection, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. See hypotheses for four ranked contrarian detectors (knickpoint swarm, worm persistence, strain-gradient Laplacian, radiometric×drainage) — ranked by expected DTI vs cost, all testable without private data except DEM/radiometrics from public 3DEP/ScienceBase.
