Submission: exact steps

  1. Download the official competition bundle while signed in to DrivenData; place it in the local data/ directory. Do not commit the large data files.
  2. Restore the shared feature stack, buffered-segment holdout evaluator, and submission writer. Run leakage canaries and spatial validation before using a submission slot.
  3. Compare the candidate with registry rasters; then write the single-band GeoTIFF from a validated prediction, matching the official sample CRS, dimensions, affine transform, and footprint.
  4. Run python scripts/validate_submission.py OUTPUT.tif --template OFFICIAL_SAMPLE.tif. This checks format/range/alignment only; passing is not a holdout score.
  5. Only after a successful validator report and positive holdout result should the artifact be clearly labelled READY TO DOWNLOAD. On DrivenData's New submission form upload that .tif, enter its unique name and a comment of no more than 140 characters, and retain the organizer receipt.

Research queue — hypotheses, not validated results

1 · tentative priority

Drainage-network deflection / catchment imbalance

DEM-derived channel azimuth and opposing catchment asymmetry may capture structure-controlled drainage beyond mapped traces. Mimics: lithology, landslides, roads, and erosional capture. Must distinguish from hydro-conduit submissions in the supplied record.

2

Aligned channel-profile knickpoints

Coherent longitudinal-profile breaks on nearby channels may signal structural uplift or permeability contrast. Mimics: resistant strata, glacial erosion, dams, and DEM artifacts.

3

Scale-persistent magnetic edges

Stable edge geometry across scales could identify concealed susceptibility boundaries. Mimics: intrusive contacts, alteration, cultural sources, and survey seams. High duplicate risk versus listed magnetic/tilt methods.

4

Radiometric-boundary × drainage interaction

Test compositional boundary coincidence with drainage changes rather than a standalone radiometric score. Mimics: lithologic contacts, soil/vegetation moisture, and survey artifacts. Related fusion/hydro work is already listed.
