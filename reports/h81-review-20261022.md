## Why H33-2-B2 was cited at 0.2778, and can we beat it?
DTI (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel) pays only the **max** kernel
credit per truth pixel but charges every positive pixel FP weight. H33-2-B2
is a pruning of a dotted ridge to 37,654 off-catalogue dots — high credit
per pixel, not a new detector (repo forensic autopsy:
`evidence/h33_forensic_autopsy.json`). 0.2778 and leaderboard-top 0.4413 are
owner/user-reported in the standing prompt, **not** organizer receipts in
this repo. Beating 0.2778 live requires higher credit density on truly
hidden faults; holdout DTI is a catalogue hide-and-recover proxy and cannot
prove a live score. No projection is written as a score.
